# NOVELTY / LINEAGE NOTE

This hypothesis was designed after publicly discussed same-retailer multiple manual jackpot cases. It is therefore `POST_LINEAGE_EXPLORATORY`, not independent confirmatory evidence. The experiment studies retailer/mode crowd behavior, not DRAW probability, number selection quality, or same-person identity.

The conditional randomization fixes each round's retailer multiplicities and total AUTO/MANUAL counts, then randomizes only mode labels. Novelty is `NOVELTY_NOT_CONFIRMED`. Even a positive result has maximum status `EXPLORATORY_RETAILER_MODE_CONCENTRATION`; promotion and official-engine effects are forbidden without prospective or independent external evidence.
