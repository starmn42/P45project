# METHODOLOGY AND NOVELTY NOTE

This is a post-lineage exploratory omnibus test. It asks whether prize-tier counts depart from geometry-free exchangeability across the Johnson J(45,6) distance shells after reconstructing radial pair intensities from factorial cross moments. It does not identify a topology-specific mechanism and cannot become an official DRAW-engine rule from this run.

The design is distinct from EXP-CROWD-TOPO-001 local D1 autocorrelation and EXP-CROWD-TOPO-002 random-column overdispersion. It reconstructs all seven radial shells and tests five preregistered normalized contrasts jointly. Its maximum positive label is `EXPLORATORY_TOPOLOGY_DEVIATION`.

No outcome statistic was inspected before the source preflight, combinatorial coefficient proof, rank proof, and locked protocol were written.
